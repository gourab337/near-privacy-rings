What you can do

Private, by proof. Not by promise.

Private sends

No relayer. No server.

Your browser makes a zero-knowledge proof in about seven seconds, and the NEAR contract verifies it. Sender, recipient and amount stay hidden. Even the person you pay can't tell who sent it.

Private swaps on Rhea

Real liquidity, hidden trader.

A single-use account, paid through the open ring, swaps on Rhea and deposits the result back as a private note. The swap is public; who made it isn't.

Peer-to-peer swaps

Both sides, one transaction.

Trade directly with someone by offer code. Both legs settle atomically, with both tokens and both amounts hidden.

Private AI agents

Private inference. Private payments.

Give an AI agent its own private wallet. Its model runs in a TEE on NEAR AI Cloud, and we check the Intel TDX attestation ourselves. Spending limits live in code, not in the model.

Deposits from any chain · payment links

No NEAR account. No bridge.

Fund a private balance with USDC on Ethereum, Base, Arbitrum or Solana, USDT, SOL, ETH or BTC. NEAR Intents swaps it and delivers it to the pool with a proof made in your browser. Or share a payment link: whoever opens it pays you privately, with no wallet in the app.

Why NEAR

Anonymous. No relayer.

On Solana

An anonymous transaction needs a relayer. A fee payer's key can spend its whole balance, so it can't be shared. The relayer has to be online, and can refuse you.

On NEAR

A key can be limited to one method on one contract. So we publish it: anyone signs, nobody can move funds, and no relayer is needed.

Roadmap

What ships next.

Independent audit

Reviewed by AI agents so far. A professional audit comes before the deposit caps go up.

Multi-party ceremony

A public trusted-setup ceremony with many contributors replaces today's single-party proving key.

Quantum-safe private wallet

A STARK-based pool: no trusted setup, and spending that a quantum computer cannot forge.

Higher caps, then immutable

Deposits start at about 50 NEAR worth per token. Caps rise after the audit, then the upgrade key is dropped.
